FAQ

How-to questions are in Help.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan and no locked feature. The code is MIT licensed.

Do I need an account?

No. Guests can upload and get a private delete link. A free HOP ID adds a gallery, albums and higher limits, and also signs you in to LinkHop, BioHop and NoteHop.

Is my location visible in my photos?

No. GPS position and camera details are removed from every upload before it is stored.

How long are files kept?

Forever, unless you choose an expiry when uploading or delete them yourself.

Do you show ads or track me?

No ads and no third-party trackers. See Privacy.

Can I host my own PixelHop?

Yes: one Go binary and one SQLite file. See the README on GitHub.

How do I report an image?

Press Report on the image page. Copyright notices: takedowns.

Is it working right now?

See the status page.
