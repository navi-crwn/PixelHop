Terms, privacy & takedowns

Short and plain. By uploading to {{.Site}} you agree to the rules below.

The rules

  • Only upload images you have the right to share.
  • No child sexual abuse material, ever. It is removed, and reported to the authorities.
  • No sexual or graphic images of people who didn't agree to it, no doxxing, no harassment.
  • No malware, phishing, spam or scams.
  • Images that break these rules are removed, and the account or address that uploaded them may be blocked.
  • The service is free and comes with no guarantee. Keep your own copies of anything important.

Privacy

  • Hidden photo data is removed. GPS location, camera serial numbers and similar EXIF, XMP and IPTC data are stripped from every upload.
  • What we keep: the image, its size and shape, the time of upload, a view counter (views per day, and the host name of sites that link to it, like reddit.com, never the full address or who viewed it), and the IP address it came from (to fight abuse).
  • Accounts store your email, your name if you give one, and a securely hashed password.
  • No trackers. There are no ads, analytics or tracking scripts. The only cookies are the one that keeps you signed in and, when you open a password-protected image, one that remembers you unlocked it.
  • Videos and PDFs. Videos have their location and other metadata removed and are re-encoded to a smaller size; PDFs are compressed; when the smaller copy is ready it replaces the original./li>
  • Webhooks. If you add a webhook, we send details of your own uploads and deletions to the address you chose, and nowhere else.
  • The studio edits images on your own device. It downloads open-source models for text reading and background removal from a public CDN the first time you use them; your image is not sent anywhere. The one exception is “Remove background → On our server”, which you choose yourself: the image is sent to this server, processed in memory and discarded, never stored.
  • Deleting an image removes it and its thumbnail from storage straight away. Deleting your account removes all your images.

Reporting an image & copyright takedowns

Every image page has a Report button; reports reach the moderators right away. For copyright (DMCA) notices, send the image link, proof that you own the work, and your contact details{{with .Data}} to {{.}}{{else}} using the Report button and choosing “My copyright”{{end}}.
