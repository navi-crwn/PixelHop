Uploading

What can I upload?

JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP and BMP, up to {{.MaxMB}} MB each and {{.MaxBatch}} at a time. Drop files, paste with Ctrl+V, or give a link to an image on the web.

How do I share many images at once?

Upload them together: they become one album with its own link. Under the list, Copy all links copies every direct link, page link, Markdown, BBCode or HTML in one go. Album pages have the same buttons.

How long do images stay online?

Forever, unless you pick Delete after when uploading, or delete them yourself.

How do I delete an image I uploaded as a guest?

Each guest upload gets a private delete link, also saved in your browser under “Uploaded from this device” on the home page. Signed-in users delete from My images.

Links

Which link should I use?

View opens the image page (good for chats). Direct is the image file itself (for forums, Markdown and websites). Thumb is a small preview. All of them are on the image page.

Do old p.hel.ink links still work?

Yes. Page links, direct /i/… links in every size, and upload result links all keep working.

Studio

Is my image uploaded when I edit it?

No for compress, resize, crop, convert, adjust, blur, palette, text reading and “Remove background → On this device”. Server tools (background removal on the server, face blur, magic eraser, HD upscale) send the image to this server, process it in memory and discard it.

Why do some tools need an account?

The AI tools that run on our server cost real computing power, so they're for signed-in users with a daily limit. Everything else works without an account.

Can I edit many images at once?

Yes: Batch resizes, converts, removes backgrounds, blurs faces or upscales a whole set, then gives you a ZIP or one album.

Privacy & safety

Is my location removed?

Yes. GPS position, camera details and similar hidden data are stripped from every upload, without lowering the quality.
